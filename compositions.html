Compositions

Michael is an avid composer and occasional arranger. He first studied composition as a boy at school, before continuing his studies at university with Dr Piers Hellawell. His work is predominantly choral, though he also enjoys writing chamber music for instrumentalists.

For scores, performance materials, or enquiries about performing any of these works, please get in touch via the Contact page.
