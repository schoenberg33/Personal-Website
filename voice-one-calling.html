A Voice of One Calling

SATB (with divisi) & organ
5′

An Advent setting of Isaiah’s prophecy in Chapter 40 (‘In the wilderness prepare the way of the Lord; ...’). This work moves from the desolation of the ‘wilderness’ to the joyful expectation of Christ’s coming.

A Voice of One Calling won third place at the ESAF Young Composers’ Competition in August 2024, adjudicated by Dame Judith Weir.

Premiere: 7 December 2024, St Salvator’s Chapel Choir, dir. Claire Innes-Hopkins, University of St Andrews’ Annual Carol Service
