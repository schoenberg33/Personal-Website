PSALM 150, with Trumpets and Drums

SATB (with divisi), organ, two trumpets, timpani 5′30″

A festal setting of this joyful psalm, written for Portsmouth Cathedral Choir and Dr David Price to celebrate his thirtieth year in post.

Unashamedly inspired by the ‘Praise ye the gods’ section of Walton’s Belshazzar’s Feast, the setting brings the psalm’s instruments to life in turn through trumpets, timpani and the varied colours of the organ.
